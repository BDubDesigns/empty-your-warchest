# Character credits

The siege's guard and captain, and their clips. All CC0 (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Built by tools/characters/build.py from Quaternius's Source editions (bought), read as data from their glTF exports: each character's parts merged into one skinned mesh and cut to each tier's triangles (High and Low), colour maps only (normal, roughness and ORM maps, vertex colours and extra UV sets dropped) at 1024, 512 and 256 px, the skeleton without its leaf bones, and the clips cut to what moves.
